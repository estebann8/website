About

I've spent the last seven years designing tools for makers — woodworkers, potters, small manufacturers — most recently leading product design at a shop-management startup. I care about software that respects the loud, dusty, hands-full reality of a workshop, not just the quiet of an office.

Before that I trained as a cabinetmaker, which is where the carpentry habit started. I still build most of my own furniture, and I write occasionally about the overlap between craft and software.
